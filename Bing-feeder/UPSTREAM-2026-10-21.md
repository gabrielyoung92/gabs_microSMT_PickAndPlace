# MicroSMT feeder update — 21 October 2026

Original designs: [MicroSMT / Bing-feeder](https://github.com/microsmt/Bing-feeder).
[Credits and local changes](../CREDITS.md).

- [V485 feeders](V485-feeder/README.md): added 12, 16 and 24 mm parts and a shared-parts gallery; updated the 8 mm motor support and 55T gear. The older 8 mm gear remains labelled as a legacy variant.
- [V3 feeders](V3-feeder/README.md): updated the wider gear brackets and 24 mm trigger, brought the existing 8 mm holders/bracket up to the supplied release, and added the 12 mm switch-cover 3MF.
- New and changed models have matching STL exports, a main JPG and eight isometric views. Existing names, reference photos and gallery style are retained.
- Firmware, Gerbers, schematics, BOMs, recovered KiCad projects and local improvements are preserved. The latest V485 archive contains CAD only.
- No dedicated 16 mm V485 feeder-slot model is supplied. CAD availability and previews do not establish physical fit.
