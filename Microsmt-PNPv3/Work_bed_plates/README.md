# Work-bed plates

Drawings by **MicroSMT**, supplied on 9 November 2026. The machine uses three
standard plates and one plate with a cut-out for the bottom camera.

- [Two-page PDF: drawings, dimensions and hole centres](PNPv3-work-bed-plates.pdf)
- Original CAD: [1.DWG — standard plate](1.DWG), [2.DWG — camera plate](2.DWG)
- [Hole-centre coordinates (CSV)](hole-centres.csv)

| Drawing | Plate | Quantity per machine | Size | M3 tapped holes | Ø3.5 clearance holes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Standard | 3 | 125 × 255 × 3 mm | 12 | 6 |
| 2 | Camera | 1 | 125 × 255 × 3 mm, with a 60 × 74 mm corner cut-out | 10 | 5 |

Both are 6061 aluminium. The drawing specifies a sandblasted, matte black
anodised finish. The original DWGs are unchanged.

## Hole positions

All coordinates are in millimetres, measured from the lower-left of the full
125 × 255 mm rectangle in the drawing. On the camera plate, this corner is
inside the cut-out. These are drawing coordinates, not OpenPnP machine positions.

The holes are not on a uniform grid. The two main M3 columns are 62.5 mm apart;
use the PDF tables or CSV for every centre.

## Drawing notes

Drawing 1 includes a separate **132.79** annotation above its **125 mm** width
dimension. The actual outline is 125 mm wide. Both annotations are retained in
the PDF so this source discrepancy is visible.

The material notes say **120PCS** and **40PCS**. Those are batch quantities;
the machine uses **three standard plates and one camera plate**.
